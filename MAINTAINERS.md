# Maintainers — mama-framework

**Status:** draft

Every MAMA repository names at least one accountable maintainer. Until the Stewardship Council is fully seated, the initial maintainers below hold decision rights per [GOVERNANCE.md §3](https://github.com/Measuring-Automation-Managing-Autonomy/.github/blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md#3-decision-rights), with public records and 7-day comment windows.

## Conflicts of interest

Declared conflicts are listed here and kept current. None declared at v0.1.0.

## Succession

Per the org governance policy: maintainers are expected to name a successor and keep this file current. A repository without an active maintainer for 90 days is labeled `deprecated` in the org profile until re-stewarded.

## Becoming a maintainer

Sustained, substantive contribution to framework decisions (issues, ADRs, reviews) plus agreement to the governance rules. Propose via a Framework Proposal issue.
